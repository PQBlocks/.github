# Code of conduct

PQBlocks adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and keep discussions technical. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in any PQBlocks space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the [PQBlocks organisation page](https://github.com/PQBlocks). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or contributors that violate this code.
